# Home — Ruleta de la suerte

**Scope:** `index.html` + `en/index.html` (shared `styles.css`, `app.js`, `en/app.js`).
**Mode:** Operate. The visitor's job is to get an answer in seconds; the page's craft has to make that instrument feel considered, not gamified.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The wheel as a plate in a printed almanac. A masthead, a ruled page, flat spot inks and a signed verdict replace the category's dark glass, neon halos and gradient text. The page refuses to look like a casino widget.

**OWN-WORLD:** Warm ivory paper and near-black ink by day; the same page printed on ink by night. Vermilion as the only shout — the italic word in the headline, the rules, the seal on the verdict. One-pixel rules, double rule under the masthead, a dashed perforation on the verdict slip, printed spot-colour wheel with graduation ticks. Bodoni Moda carries display and verdict; Karla carries the interface. Square-ish corners, no glass, no glow, no grain filters.

**STORY:** This is an almanac that signs verdicts. Write what is at stake, turn the wheel, receive the entry. Playful, but composed enough to trust.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Masthead (wordmark + tagline, dateline, theme/language/sound controls) over a double rule; a left-aligned two-line headline with the italic vermilion word; a narrow editorial lede on the right separated by a hairline column rule. Below, the wheel as a large plate on the left with a `Fig. 1` caption, and on the right a single ruled column: verdict slip, options ledger, latest verdicts. No cards, no hero pill.

**FORM:** Editorial almanac / printed gazette. Top-ranked of the seven structures derived from the audience's print world (almanac, ticket, casino chip, game-show scoreboard, lottery slip, newspaper, art-deco poster). `seed: none (unavailable)`.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
